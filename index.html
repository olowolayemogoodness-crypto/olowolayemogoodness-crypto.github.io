Edulink

Every campus, in one app.

Campus life in Nigeria runs on scattered WhatsApp groups. Exam dates, hostel news and class updates get lost in hundreds of messages, and there's no single place to see what's happening on your own campus.

Edulink fixes that. You pick your university when you sign up, and your feed fills with posts from students on your campus: news, photos, videos, voice notes and banter. Follow people, chat privately, and join communities built around what you love.

  • Campus feedPhotos, videos that play as you scroll, link previews.
  • CommunitiesBooks, gaming, tech, anime, music, each with a chat room.
  • Founding badgesThe first 1,000 students get a numbered badge for life.
  • CloutPoints for being active and for bringing friends along.
built withFlutter · Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Functions, Messaging, Hosting) · Cloudflare Workers & R2 · Google & Apple Sign-In

Blink

Real conversations. Real connection.

My family talks on WhatsApp, but our video calls kept breaking up and the conversations that mattered got buried. Staying close needs more than a chat list.

Blink is a private messenger I built so we don't drift apart. It does the basics well, and adds small things that keep people talking every day.

  • Chats & voice notesText, photos, videos and voice. Unsend within 15 minutes.
  • Clearer callsVoice and video built to beat our WhatsApp calls. Never recorded.
  • Streaks & pulseSee how often you keep in touch with each person.
  • Stories & imports24-hour stories, and bring old WhatsApp chats along.

No ads, no selling data, no access to your contacts or location.

built withFirebase · LiveKit for voice & video · Cloudflare R2 for media · Push notifications

GO

Put a fee on your commitment. Get all of it back.

Students and developers set goals, then lose them to scrolling and late nights. Ticking a box is easy, and nothing is lost when you quit.

GO gives your habits something to lose. You put down a commitment fee, prove your habits every day for 30 days, and get the whole fee back when you follow through. The money is simulated, so you feel the stakes with no real risk.

  1. Answer four questions so GO knows when you work best and what derails you.
  2. Pick up to five habits: push a commit, screen time, workout, study, reading, phone down by 11pm.
  3. Commit the fee and name a partner who can overrule the checker.
  4. Charge the battery daily. GitHub checks your code, a code word in your photo proves camera habits, and a page counts after 12 seconds of real reading.
  5. Win in 30 days with 90% done and 3 grace passes, and the full fee comes back.
trackTaskforge student hackathon: accessible tools that help people focus and get more done

Ecograph

Count every tonne once.

Companies buy carbon credits to make up for their pollution, and each credit should stand for one tonne of carbon, counted once. But one credit can be claimed by two companies, two projects can claim the same forest, and credits can circle between sellers to fake demand.

Ecograph reads the whole market as a map of connections between land, projects, credits and buyers. It finds those tricks, groups related warnings into one case, and scores each case with the reasons spelled out. The AI only suggests. A person decides to freeze, refer or dismiss, and every decision is logged.

Hustle

Work near you. Get paid securely.

Millions of Nigerians earn a living from informal work: movers, plumbers, drivers, cooks. Many wait at junctions hoping someone hires them, lose money to middlemen, and sometimes never get paid. With no record of their work, banks won't lend to them.

Hustle connects informal workers with people who need a job done nearby, and protects the money for both sides.

  • Jobs on a mapWork within 1 to 10 km of where you are.
  • Paid for sureMoney is locked in escrow and released when the job is done.
  • Trust scoreBuilt from ratings, finished jobs and ID checks.
  • Keep 100%Workers keep the full price. Employers pay a 2% fee on top.
built withFlutter · Riverpod · FastAPI (Python) · Supabase with PostGIS · Squad API by GTBank · OpenStreetMap

Ajo

Group savings you can trust.

Ajo, or esusu, is how many Nigerians save. A group each pays the same amount every week or month, and each round one person takes the whole pot. It runs on trust, and trust breaks easily: someone pays late, the notebook is wrong, or nobody is sure whose turn is next.

Ajo moves the savings circle into an app, so everyone sees the same record of who paid, whose turn it is and what's due next, and each member can keep track of their own money.

  • Invite-only circlesSet the amount, how often and how many people.
  • The ajo wheelShows who gets the pot, and when.
  • Reminders & nudgesBefore a payment is due, and for late friends.
  • Ajo AI in PidginAsk about your circle in Naija Pidgin.

Prototype: payments are simulated and no real money moves.

built withFlutter · Firebase · Google Sign-In · Pidgin assistant · Simulated payments
